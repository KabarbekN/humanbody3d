## Visible Human Project

The U.S. National Library of Medicine Visible Human Project was consulted as a full-color gross-anatomy reference for the visualization palette:

- https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/visible/visible_human.html

No Visible Human image data are bundled in this project.

## Important limitation

The source STL files contain geometry but no physically measured tissue colors or photographic textures. The colors, roughness and translucency in this viewer are artistic/educational visualization choices and must not be interpreted as diagnostic data.
